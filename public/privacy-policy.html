Runeveil Odyssey: Idle RPG

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Runeveil Odyssey: Idle RPG handles information when you use the Android game published by the Runeveil Odyssey developer account.

Information stored by the game

Your game progress, settings, and local purchase state are stored on your device. The game does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or photos.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Google may use this information for personalized or non-personalized ads according to your device and consent choices.

You can purchase the one-time Remove Ads product through Google Play. When the purchase is active, the game hides its ad placements. Google Play processes the transaction; the game does not receive your payment card details.

Google Play Billing

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. The game stores only the local entitlement needed to restore the Remove Ads purchase on that device and Google Play account. We do not receive or store payment credentials.

Children

Runeveil Odyssey is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is published. For privacy questions, please use the project’s public support channel at GitHub Issues.
